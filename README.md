###  Featured Project: FarmTrack
**FarmTrack** is a full-stack Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to empower livestock & poultry farmers across Africa with real-time tracking, AI diagnostics, and financial management.

- **Frontend & App Architecture:** Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, PWA setup.
- **Backend & Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Row Level Security, Realtime subscriptions).
- **AI Integration:** AI Veterinary Consultant & automated unprompted mortality risk alerts.
- **Payments & Commerce:** Paystack integration for farm subscriptions & transaction processing.
- **Live Demo:** [farm-track.taupe.vercel.app](https://farm-track.taupe.vercel.app)
